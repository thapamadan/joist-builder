Frequently Asked Question

You May Ask

Can you handle design and construction together?
Yes. We can connect planning, drawings, 3D visualization, estimation, site work, and finishing so the project stays clear from start to handover.
Do you provide 3D views before construction?
Yes. 3D design helps you understand space, elevation, interior feel, and material direction before major construction decisions are made.
Can you renovate existing houses?
Yes. We review the existing condition first, then suggest practical renovation, extension, repair, or remodeling options.
Do you do surveying and valuation?
Yes. We support site surveying, measurement, estimation, and property valuation depending on the project requirement.
How do I start?
Contact us with your location, project type, and basic requirement. We will discuss the scope and suggest the next step.
Do you work outside Pokhara?
We are based in Pokhara and can discuss projects in other parts of Nepal depending on scope, schedule, and site needs.
Can you estimate construction cost?
Yes. We prepare practical cost guidance based on drawings, material choices, structure, finishing level, and site conditions.
Do you help with local approvals?
We can guide drawings and documentation needed for approval. Exact requirements depend on municipality and project location.
Do you provide interior design?
Yes. We plan interiors with layouts, lighting, material mood, storage, furniture placement, and everyday usability in mind.
Can you inspect an existing structure?
Yes. We can review visible site conditions and advise whether more detailed structural analysis is required.
